01 / The person behind the practice

Order. Balance.
Room to grow.

Stability with an open mind.

A man seeking order and balance, wherever possible, in the many aspects of life.

Stability need not mean inflexibility. I aim to learn from others and improve myself, for my own sake and for the people around me.

I prioritise both work and leisure. A machine is only as efficient as its maintenance, and healthy hobbies are an essential part of that balance.

Read my full introduction +

I enjoy a combination of hobbies that offer expression, enjoyment and improvement.

I have studied art through school classes, tuition classes and self-directed learning. I take commissions as a freelance artist as a side gig, but more importantly as a challenge to fulfil clients’ requirements.

I enjoy games that involve strategy and quick decision-making, keeping my mind sharp and developing my thinking skills.

Among my other hobbies, swimming is both a healthy pursuit and a vital survival skill. I also freelance in voice acting, as a way to express myself and improve my vocal control and theatrics.
